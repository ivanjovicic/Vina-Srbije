## Research limitations

- No authoritative current 2026 public number of wine-tourism visitors was found.
- No confirmed reusable public API/export of the Serbian Vinski registar was found in this research pass.
- Current winery direct-site sample is directional, not statistically representative.
- Exact fiscalization/payment/tourism classification for proposed transaction flows requires professional legal/accounting advice.
- Courier alcohol/age-verification economics were not contracted/quoted and therefore are intentionally excluded from commerce projections.
- No Serbia-specific no/low-alcohol or generational wine-consumption dataset was found; global trend evidence must not be projected directly onto Serbia.

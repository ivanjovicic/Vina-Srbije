# Competitive Landscape

## 1. Executive conclusion

Konkurencija je jača nego što “Vinski putevi Srbije” naziv sugeriše. Gotovo svaki pojedinačni feature već postoji negde. Potencijal nije u feature parity-ju već u **lokalnom workflow-u koji konkurenti ne povezuju dobro: verified visit -> tasting memory -> repeat action -> measurable winery ROI**.

## 3. VinarijeSrbije

Current positioning: **430 vinarija, 22 rejona, 3 regije**, interaktivna mapa, nearby discovery, degustacije/smeštaj, kontakt i planer vinske rute. [S01]

### Strengths

- vrlo široka lokalna pokrivenost;
- SEO relevance za Srbiju;
- već rešava “gde su vinarije?”;
- route planning smanjuje prostor za basic itinerary feature.

### Weakness / whitespace hypotheses

- nema dokaziv transactional loop;
- nema jak post-visit retention/reorder layer;
- nema jasan attributed winery ROI.

**Implication:** ne takmičiti se brojem listinga.

## 4. SerbianWine

SerbianWine već pozicionira proizvod kao multilingual wine-country guide sa 100+ vinarija, mapom, wine routes, event kalendarom, restoranima, smeštajem i tastings. Na stranici degustacija eksplicitno savetuje direktan kontakt i reconfirm availability; bookable experiences vodi preko Viator affiliate linka. [S02][S17]

### Strengths

- veoma blizu “Wine Serbia tourism content” viziji;
- English/Russian;
- curated route/storytelling;
- affiliate model bez payment complexity.

### Strategic lesson

Jednostavna kombinacija “multilingual + route + map + tasting” nije diferencijacija.

## 5. Vivino

Vivino trenutno prikazuje **433 Serbia wineries**, ratings i hiljade user signals za poznate proizvođače. [S03]

### Incumbent advantages

- ogromna globalna wine graph baza;
- label/ratings behavior;
- brand awareness;
- network effects u review data;
- mature recommendation/discovery.

### Gde ne treba napadati

- general wine ratings;
- label scanning kao glavni feature;
- global consumer taste graph;
- “najbolje vino” crowd ranking.

### Mogući lokalni whitespace

- tourism operations;
- verified local experience availability;
- Serbian winery visit attribution;
- local repeat-purchase bridge.

**Attack test:** ako Vivino doda tourism booking, naš moat mora ostati winery relationship + local operational data + offline QR/attribution, ne samo UI.

## 6. WineTourism.com

WineTourism trenutno prodaje konkretna iskustva u Srbiji, sa primerima €9, €12, €20, €25, €30, €45, €60, €65. [S05]

Partner economics globalno: oko **20%** na reservation-upon-confirmation i **15%** za instant confirmation, uz minimum €5 po osobi. [S06]

### Strengths

- actual booking, ne samo content;
- payment/cancellation workflow;
- existing SEO/global tourist demand;
- supply partner model.

### Weakness in Serbia — hypothesis

- supply coverage je znatno manja od ukupnog broja tourism-ready vinarija;
- može biti manje lokalnog content/data depth-a;
- nema prirodan post-visit Serbian reorder/loyalty loop.

### Implication

Booking sam po sebi nije dovoljan. Lokalni proizvod mora imati ili bolju supply penetration ili drugačiji lifetime relationship.

## 7. Wine Vision

Wine Vision 2026 je 10–12. oktobra u Beogradu. Organizator prikazuje scale od 535 exhibitors, 34 zemlje, 302 professional buyers i 1.500+ business meetings, dok B2B program koristi profile/interesovanja, product catalogue i feedback signale za matchmaking koji se prenose u naredni ciklus. [S07][S25]

### Strengths

- koncentracija supply + buyers;
- regional authority;
- product-level B2B data;
- year-over-year relationship;
- awards and professional community.

### Implication

Ne praviti “digitalni sajam/importer catalogue” bez posebno jakog year-round problem statement-a.

## 8. Winalist / CellarPass / Tock

### Winalist

Winalist navodi 2.050+ pažljivo odabranih vinarija i destilerija i pozicionira se kao consumer discovery + booking layer, uz modele kao što su booking/pay-on-arrival i free cancellation. To je važan dokaz da „wine experience marketplace“ nije nova kategorija; lokalna prednost mora biti supply density, trust/data ili post-visit loop. [S28]

### CellarPass

CellarPass je specijalizovan winery reservations/event/ticketing proizvod. Aktuelni planovi počinju oko $79 mesečno, a platforma kombinuje reservations, prepaid experiences, integrations i acquisition/discovery. Njihov positioning eksplicitno povezuje booking sa wine-club/DTC lifetime value-om. To dodatno pojačava zaključak da booking-only ili CRM-lite nisu whitespace. [S29]

### Tock

Tock ima winery-specific reservations/experience positioning, guest profiles, prepayment/deposits i integracije sa Commerce7/WineDirect. Platforma navodi hiljade hospitality lokacija i snažno rešava „visitor -> regular“ problem. Ako lokalni B2B wedge bude guest retention, mora pobediti lokalnim distribution/payment/fiscal/tourism kontekstom, ne feature breadth-om. [S30]

### Strategic implication

Globalni benchmark kaže da su tri use-case-a već zrela:

1. discovery/booking;
2. tasting-room operations;
3. guest CRM/retention.

Naš wedge može biti održiv samo ako lokalna kombinacija **distribution + verified Serbia data + QR/offline attribution + winery ROI** smanjuje trošak ili povećava prihod na način koji se ne dobija samo kupovinom stranog SaaS-a.

## 9. Winery DTC software: Commerce7 i WineDirect

Commerce7 trenutno nudi CRM, clubs/subscriptions, ecommerce, POS/reservations i analytics od $59/mesečno u Lite planu; više planove $149, $399 i $1.249 uz transaction fees na nekim tier-ovima. [S12]

WineDirect DTC Starter je $79/mesečno + 2% transaction fee, a feature set uključuje ecommerce, CRM, tasting-room/POS i wine club. [S13]

### Implication

Generički “winery CRM + ecommerce + booking” je zrela međunarodna kategorija. Ako se radi B2B softver za Srbiju, wedge mora biti lokalni:

- domaća plaćanja;
- fiskalni workflow;
- veoma jednostavan UX;
- tourism demand generation;
- QR repeat purchase;
- Serbian/English content;
- lokalna cena.

## 10. Substitute: direct winery digital

Leading local wineries već imaju solidan direct stack:

- Zvonko Bogdan: tasting paketi i reservation/contact;
- Kovačević: online wine catalogue i rezervacija ture;
- Aleksandrović: web-shop + wine tourism/food pairing;
- Jeremić/Deurić: online shop;
- Deurić: objavljene tasting/tour cene.

To znači da pitch “napravićemo vinariji online prodaju” nije dovoljno jak za bolji deo tržišta.

## 11. Competitive whitespace map

### Crveno — ne ulaziti kao core

- directory/map;
- generic reviews;
- label scan;
- global taste ratings;
- generic winery ecommerce/CRM;
- generic B2B fair matching.

### Žuto — moguće uz lokalnu prednost

- booking;
- curated route;
- occasion/taste discovery;
- winery analytics;
- events.

### Zeleno — najzanimljivije hipoteze

- verified tasting -> digital wine memory;
- offline attribution;
- repeat purchase bridge;
- measurable winery ROI;
- local tourism data freshness;
- QR distribution through partner wineries.

## 12. Why not existing alternatives?

### Why not Google?

Google je bolji za place discovery i directions. Naš proizvod ima smisla samo ako nudi pouzdanije experience data + memory/reorder workflow.

### Why not Vivino?

Vivino je bolji za global wine ratings/taste. Naš proizvod ima smisla samo ako poseduje lokalni winery experience i post-visit relationship.

### Why not VinarijeSrbije/SerbianWine?

Oni već dobro rešavaju discovery/content. Naš proizvod mora dokazati transactional/retention ROI, ne novi layout.

### Why not WineTourism/Viator?

Oni su bolji za booking marketplace. Lokalna prednost mora biti širi supply + niži friction + post-visit retention/reorder + winery attribution.

## 13. Najjači competitor nije jedna firma

Najčešći realni konkurent je:

**Google Maps + Instagram + winery phone/WhatsApp + Vivino + winery web-shop.**

Da bi novi proizvod pobedio, mora skratiti proces i proizvesti vrednost koju ovaj stack ne daje.

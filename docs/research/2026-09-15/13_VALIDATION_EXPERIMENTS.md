# Validation Experiments

## Principle

The next milestone is not a production launch. It is reducing uncertainty cheaply.

Thresholds below are hypotheses selected to force decisions; they are not industry benchmarks.

## Experiment V1 — Winery pain interviews

**Hypothesis:** acquisition, booking friction or repeat purchase is a top-3 commercial problem for a meaningful subset of tourism-ready wineries.

**Sample:** 20–30 wineries across Srem and Šumadija; mix of size/digital maturity.  
**Cost:** low, founder time/travel.  
**Duration:** 2–3 weeks; accelerate at Wine Vision.  
**Positive:** >=30–40% independently name one target problem as top-3 + >=5 pilot commitments.  
**Negative:** <20% see problem and almost all are satisfied with current channels.  
**If pass:** proceed to pilot.  
**If fail:** test corporate gifting/HORECA or stop.

## Experiment V2 — Consumer workflow interviews

**Hypothesis:** users experience meaningful friction in discovering/confirming winery visits and remembering/reordering wines.

**Sample:** 15 domestic + 10 foreign or recent foreign-tourist proxy interviews.  
**Positive:** >=40% report multi-source/booking friction; >=30% have failed to remember/rebuy a liked wine.  
**Negative:** users consistently solve problem in minutes with Google/direct and do not care about memory/reorder.

## Experiment V3 — Manual wine-day concierge

**Hypothesis:** curated, verified plan materially improves decision and drives winery action.

**Method:** manually create 20 personalized day plans; no route engine.  
**Measure:** plan accepted, winery contacted, actual visit, planning time, feedback.  
**Positive:** >=40% qualified users perform winery action and >=25% say existing process was meaningfully painful.  
**Failure:** <20% action or most users prefer self-service Google flow.

## Experiment V4 — QR Wine Passport

**Hypothesis:** tasting visitors will save a specific wine if friction is very low.

**Supply:** 3–5 wineries.  
**Method:** physical QR on tasting table/menu; mobile web, no app install.  
**Funnel:** QR view -> wine save -> optional account/email -> return.  
**Positive:** >=30% of exposed tasting guests save at least one wine.  
**Strong:** >=15% have meaningful return/reorder content interaction within 60 days.  
**Kill Passport as core:** <15% scan/save even with visible staff-supported prompt.

## Experiment V5 — Repeat-purchase bridge

**Hypothesis:** post-tasting memory can generate later buying intent.

**Method:** consented user gets reminder 30–60 days later with exact saved vintage and direct winery order/contact path.  
**Positive:** >=10% click qualified reorder action; >=3–5% confirmed order/serious inquiry would be promising at small sample.  
**Important:** actual thresholds must be recalibrated by product price/frequency/sample.

## Experiment V6 — Booking lead pilot

**Hypothesis:** structured lead/request improves conversion and wineries will value it.

**Supply:** 5–10 wineries.  
**Measure:** profile views -> request -> accepted -> attended; response time.  
**Positive:** high-intent profile sessions produce >=10% qualified request; median response <24h; at least 3 wineries express willingness to pay after result.  
**Negative:** most requests route direct/outside platform and suppliers see no incremental value.

## Experiment V7 — Occasion vs Taste UX

**Hypothesis:** novice users choose more easily using occasion/food/budget than technical taste sliders.

**Method:** two manual/landing variants.  
A: body/tannin/acidity.  
B: occasion/food/price/intensity.  
**Measure:** completion, confidence, click/save.  
**Decision:** winner becomes MVP discovery UX. No ML.

## Experiment V8 — Winery data freshness

**Hypothesis:** partner wineries can maintain a minimal set of critical fields.

**Method:** ask 10 pilot wineries to confirm/update 8 critical data fields twice in 60 days.  
**Positive:** >=80% respond within 7 days and >90% critical fields current.  
**Negative:** high chase/manual burden -> product must become managed data service or reduce dynamic claims.

## Experiment V9 — Corporate gifts manual presale

**Hypothesis:** companies will pay for curated Serbian wine gift packages.

**Method:** 3 mock packages + quote form; outbound to 20–30 companies/office managers/partners.  
**Positive:** >=3 serious quote requests or >=1 paid pilot order before software.  
**Negative:** zero commercial intent after targeted outreach.

## Experiment V10 — Paid winery conversion

Only after delivering pilot value.

Offer:

- €29–39/month OR
- pay per confirmed qualified action OR
- hybrid.

**Positive:** first 5 paying wineries without heavy discounting.  
**Negative:** “love it but won’t pay” -> business model is not proven.

## Experiment V11 — Payment/legal discovery

Before any checkout, obtain:

- written/recorded provider capability from 2+ Serbian acquiring/PSP options;
- accountant memo on money/fiscal flow;
- legal memo on platform role/alcohol/tourism.

Success is not code. Success is a viable money-flow diagram with named providers and responsibilities.

## Experiment V12 — Commerce intent without checkout

“Buy again” can route direct to winery shop/contact. Track 50+ real purchase-intent events/month before platform cart work.

**Commerce gate opens only if:**

- intent exists;
- >=10 wineries want fulfillment;
- payment/legal model works;
- shipping quotes produce acceptable contribution.

## Validation order

1. V1 winery pain;
2. V2 consumers;
3. V3 concierge;
4. V4 Passport;
5. V5 repeat;
6. V6 lead/booking;
7. V8 freshness;
8. V10 paid conversion;
9. then payment/commerce.

Corporate gifting (V9) can run in parallel as independent revenue test.

# Gate-Based Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Due Diligence

**Status:** substantially complete through this document.  
**Goal:** identify critical uncertainties and competitive reality.  
**Exit:** decision = VALIDATE BEFORE BUILDING.

No implementation authorization.

---

## Phase 1 — Problem Validation

**Time box:** 2–3 weeks.

### Business

- 20–30 winery interviews;
- 15 domestic consumer interviews;
- 10 foreign-tourist/recent-tourist interviews;
- Wine Vision field plan.

### Product

- clickable/simple landing concepts for:
  - visit planning;
  - Passport/reorder;
  - corporate gifts.

### Engineering

- none beyond research prototypes/forms.

### Exit gate

Proceed only if:

- >=5 wineries commit to pilot;
- target pain appears repeatedly without prompting;
- consumer friction exists in at least one target job.

### Kill

No supply commitment or no meaningful pain -> PIVOT/STOP.

---

## Phase 2 — Concierge & Supply Validation

**Time box:** 2–4 weeks.

### Product

- manual winery day planning;
- manual experience requests;
- prototype Passport QR;
- manual data model spreadsheet/admin dataset.

### Data

- 10–15 verified wineries;
- critical fields + provenance;
- wine/vintage sample.

### Business

- measure response/booking;
- pilot QR placement agreement;
- time-track onboarding.

### Exit gate

- >=40% qualified concierge users perform winery action;
- >=10 wineries provide complete data;
- winery update process appears feasible.

---

## Phase 3 — QR Passport / Repeat-Purchase Proof

**Time box:** 30–60 days observational window.

### Product

- no full app required;
- mobile web QR save;
- consented follow-up;
- direct reorder/contact links.

### Metrics

- scan exposure;
- save rate;
- account/consent conversion;
- 30/60-day return;
- reorder click/inquiry/order.

### Exit gate

Hypothesis thresholds:

- >=30% exposed visitors save;
- >=15% meaningful later interaction;
- evidence of real repeat purchase intent.

### Kill

<15% save with staff-supported placement -> Passport not core.

---

## Phase 4 — Technical MVP

**Allowed only if Phases 1–3 pass.**

### Engineering

- modular monolith;
- catalog/geography/experience;
- SEO web;
- search/map;
- claim/verification;
- Passport;
- basic analytics;
- booking/request handoff;
- sr/en.

### Supply

20–30 vinarija; 100–200 wine/vintage records.

### Explicit non-scope

payments, cart, shipping, ML, mobile, public reviews.

### Exit gate

- stable data freshness;
- real qualified actions;
- user activation/repeat signals.

---

## Phase 5 — Pilot Cluster

**Duration:** 8–12 weeks.

### Regions

Fruška Gora/Srem + Šumadija/Topola.

### Metrics

- qualified sessions;
- confirmed winery actions;
- QR saves;
- repeat actions;
- partner response;
- profile freshness.

### Exit gate

At least 5 wineries have measurable ROI signal and want continued participation.

---

## Phase 6 — First Monetization

### Tests

A: €29–39/month winery plan.  
B: confirmed lead fee.  
C: hybrid subscription + lower action fee.

### Exit gate

First 5 paying wineries with acceptable acquisition/onboarding payback.

If no one pays after proven value -> re-evaluate business, not price UI.

---

## Phase 7 — Booking Workflow Expansion

Only if request booking has proven demand.

### Product

- availability windows;
- accept/reject;
- reminders;
- cancellation policy;
- possibly pay-at-winery/deposit after legal review.

### Required legal gate

Written view on tourism role + payment flow.

---

## Phase 8 — Winning Growth Layer

Choose based on evidence, not roadmap fantasy:

- more regions;
- stronger repeat purchase;
- corporate gifts;
- foreign tourist channel;
- winery analytics.

Only one primary expansion at a time.

---

## Phase 9 — Commerce Gate

Commerce remains BLOCKED until all are true:

1. >=50 monthly real purchase/reorder intent events;
2. >=10 wineries agree to fulfillment/data requirements;
3. named payment/acquiring provider supports selected structure;
4. lawyer/accountant approve merchant/fiscal/payment model;
5. courier quotes/age/breakage workflow validated;
6. unit economics positive without unrealistic shipping subsidy.

Then test **single-winery checkout first**, not multi-winery cart.

---

## Phase 10 — Mobile Gate

Native app only if:

- retained user cohort exists;
- >=30% retained users show repeated scan/push/offline needs;
- modeled retention uplift justifies second client surface.

Otherwise remain web/PWA.

---

## Phase 11 — Regional Gate

Content/tourism expansion may begin only when Serbia has:

- repeatable partner onboarding;
- >=20 active ROI-positive/paying winery relationships;
- repeatable consumer acquisition;
- stable unit economics.

Regional alcohol commerce remains separately gated by each country's tax/excise/customs rules.

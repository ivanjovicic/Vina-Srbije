# Winery Supply Analysis

## 1. Supply nije homogen

430+ listinga ne znači 430 dobrih partnera. Za pilot treba meriti **tourism readiness**, ne samo postojanje proizvođača.

To nije regulatorni source of truth, ali daje signal da postoji najmanje trocifren relevantan experience supply universe.

### Implication

Bolje vinarije već imaju direktne kanale. Platforma mora da dokaže:

- dodatni demand;
- bolji foreign discovery;
- booking conversion;
- repeat purchase;
- attribution;

ne da im ponudi još jednu administraciju.

## 5. Supply-side acquisition model

### Pilot offer

**0 RSD / free pilot 60–90 days** u zamenu za:

- verified data;
- QR placement;
- booking outcome feedback;
- monthly 15-minute feedback;
- permission to measure anonymized conversion.

### Value promise

> “Ne nudimo vam još jedan profil. Pokušavamo da dokažemo koliko gostiju i ponovnih kupovina možemo da vam pripišemo.”

### Bad pitch

> “Imamo novu aplikaciju za vinarije.”

## 6. Data-maintenance reality

Najveći supply risk je da niko u maloj vinariji nema vremena da održava:

- opening hours;
- tasting slots;
- price;
- current vintage;
- event status;
- stock.

Zato MVP treba da ima:

1. jednostavan claim workflow;
2. email/WhatsApp-style update prompt;
3. stale-data flag;
4. platform moderation;
5. last-verified date;
6. “confirm directly” fallback za HIGH-dynamic data.

## 7. Winery claim verification

Predloženi nivo:

- business-domain email ako postoji;
- official website/contact callback;
- company/official registry cross-check;
- manual admin approval;
- MFA za claimed winery account.

Ne dozvoliti instant self-claim samo preko social profile-a.

## 8. Supply metrics

- Active Verified Wineries;
- % profiles verified <90 days;
- Experience Data Freshness;
- median response time;
- qualified actions per winery;
- QR scan rate;
- repeat-purchase actions;
- monthly active winery admins;
- paying/ROI-positive winery ratio.

## 9. Supply economics

Treba meriti stvarni onboarding cost.

Formula:

`Winery Acquisition Cost = sales time + research + data entry + translation + verification + support`

Ako onboarding zahteva 4–6 sati ljudskog rada po vinariji, subscription od €20/m može imati loš payback bez višeg transaction/lead prihoda.

Zato prva 20 partnera treba detaljno time-trackovati.

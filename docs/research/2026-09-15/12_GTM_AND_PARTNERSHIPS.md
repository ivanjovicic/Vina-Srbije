# Go-To-Market & Partnerships

## 1. GTM thesis

Do not launch nationally with 430 empty/partially copied profiles. Win one dense cluster with verified data and partner behavior.

## 2. First 20 wineries

### Targeting

Prioritize wineries that have:

- tasting program;
- sufficient visitor capacity;
- public contact;
- interest in DTC/repeat purchase;
- English or willingness to serve tourists;
- owner/manager accessible;
- current digital presence but obvious booking/retention gap.

Split:

- 10 Srem/Fruška Gora;
- 8 Šumadija/Topola;
- 2 experimental outside clusters.

### Offer

60–90 day free measurable pilot:

- verified profile;
- English profile;
- experience lead flow;
- QR Wine Passport;
- analytics;
- no lock-in.

### Pitch

> “Želimo da izmerimo da li možemo da vam dovedemo novog gosta i vratimo ga ka kupovini posle degustacije. Ne tražimo da kupite softver unapred.”

### Objections

**“Već imamo Instagram/Google.”**  
Odgovor: pilot meri incremental actions/repeat, ne listing.

**“Nemamo vremena da održavamo profil.”**  
Odgovor: concierge onboarding + minimal critical update flow; istovremeno test da li problem može skalirati.

**“Ne želimo proviziju.”**  
Odgovor: pilot bez provizije; monetization tek kada postoji ROI evidence.

## 3. Wine Vision 2026 — field research plan

Datum: 10–12. oktobar 2026, Beogradski sajam. [S07]

### Pre događaja

- shortlist 40 target vinarija;
- unapred kontaktirati 15;
- QR research form;
- 10-minute interview script;
- segment score;
- daily notes template.

### Dnevni cilj

- 10–15 quality winery interviews;
- 3–5 buyer/tourism interviews;
- collect examples of actual booking/DTC workflows;
- ask for pilot permission, not opinion.

### Strong signal

- winery gives contact + agrees to pilot + provides data + agrees to QR.

### Weak signal

- “zanimljivo, javite se” bez konkretne obaveze.

## 4. First 1,000 consumer users

### Channel 1 — Winery QR

Highest-quality because user is in tasting context.

### Channel 2 — SEO intent

Focus data-rich pages:

- wineries near Belgrade/Novi Sad;
- Fruška Gora tastings;
- Šumadija day trip;
- English “wine tasting Serbia / Belgrade wine trip”.

### Channel 3 — Partners

- boutique hotels;
- concierge desks;
- local guides;
- tourism organizations;
- restaurants.

### Channel 4 — Creators

Small local food/travel creators; measure booking/save conversion, not views.

### Channel 5 — Events

Wine Vision, local festivals, open cellar days.

## 5. Foreign tourist GTM

Foreign traveler has higher trust/logistics need.

Potential entry points:

- English SEO;
- hotel concierge QR;
- travel guide partners;
- airport/city tourism content partnerships;
- affiliate/partnership rather than direct competition with every OTA.

Do not spend heavily on broad “Serbia travel” ads.

## 7. SEO strategy

Do not compete only for broad “vino” terms. Attack high-intent structured local searches where proprietary data helps.

Strong page candidates:

- region pages with real wineries/experiences;
- “degustacija + region”; 
- “winery with restaurant/accommodation”;
- English trip planning;
- native grape education linked to real places/wines.

Every indexable page must answer a real job. No 10,000 AI pages.

## 8. Referral loop

Potential loop:

winery displays QR -> visitor saves -> platform reminds -> visitor shares/reorders -> winery sees value -> winery promotes QR more.

This loop is strategically stronger than a generic consumer referral because supplier has incentive to distribute.

## 9. Brand recommendation

Do not finalize before interviews/domain/trademark check.

### “Vina Srbije”

+ local clarity/authority  
- less international/regional.

### “Wine Serbia”

+ foreign-tourist clarity  
+ B2B/export compatible  
- generic/trademark/domain challenges.

### “Vinski putevi”

+ tourism clarity  
- artificially limits commerce/repeat product.

### Independent brand

+ regional scalability  
- requires more marketing to explain.

Working recommendation: use descriptive Wine Serbia/Vina Srbije during validation; brand later.

## 10. GTM kill signal

If 20 strong winery conversations produce fewer than 5 meaningful pilot commitments, stop building consumer feature depth and reconsider the payer/problem.

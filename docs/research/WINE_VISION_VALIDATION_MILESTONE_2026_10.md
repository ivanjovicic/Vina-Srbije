# Wine Vision 2026 — Time-Sensitive Validation Milestone

Status: current validation milestone
Event dates: **10–12 October 2026**
Location: Belgrade

## Why this matters

Wine Vision is a short-lived opportunity to compress months of fragmented winery outreach into several days of concentrated field validation.

The objective is **not** to demonstrate a finished product.

The objective is to test whether the current Wine Serbia thesis deserves implementation:

> `physical tasting / winery visit -> save exact wine/vintage -> consented follow-up -> repeat action -> measurable winery ROI`

## Required preparation before the event

Prepare only validation assets:

1. one-page winery problem/value proposition;
2. clickable or static QR Wine Passport mock;
3. interview script focused on last real visitor/customer behavior, not feature opinions;
4. simple pilot commitment form;
5. list of target wineries prioritized by tasting traffic, DTC sales maturity and digital readiness;
6. evidence sheet for recording pain, current workaround, buyer, willingness to pilot, data owner and follow-up action.

Do **not** build booking, marketplace checkout, CRM, native app, logistics, or a production Passport platform for the event.

## Target outcome

During / around Wine Vision:

- 20–30 qualified winery interviews;
- at least 5 credible QR/Post-Visit pilot commitments;
- identify who owns visitor follow-up / direct sales at each pilot winery;
- confirm whether post-visit retention/reorder is a repeated unprompted pain;
- identify the existing workaround: Instagram, webshop, email, WhatsApp, CRM, wine club, manual follow-up, or none;
- capture what measurable winery outcome would justify payment.

## Evidence rules

Do not count:

- compliments;
- "interesting idea";
- requests for free directory exposure;
- business cards without an agreed next step;
- generic willingness to be listed.

Count as a credible pilot only when the winery agrees to a concrete test with:

- named contact/owner;
- test venue/context;
- approximate timing;
- staff participation or QR placement;
- permission to measure scan/save/follow-up behavior;
- post-pilot review of value.

## Go / no-go interpretation

Strong signal:
- >=5 credible pilots;
- repeated pain around post-visit customer memory/relationship/reorder;
- willingness to measure business outcomes;
- at least some willingness to discuss payment after proof.

Weak signal:
- wineries mainly want discovery/listing/publicity;
- staff will not support QR exposure;
- no owner for post-visit customer relationship;
- no practical reorder/contact path;
- <5 credible pilots after 20–30 qualified conversations.

If the signal is weak, do not respond by building a broader directory or marketplace.

## After the event

If the milestone passes:

- run 3–5 concierge QR pilots;
- measure exposure -> scan -> save;
- then measure 30/60-day follow-up/reorder/contact behavior;
- only after attributable value test actual winery willingness to pay.

If it fails:

- park product engineering;
- document the actual dominant winery pain discovered;
- reconsider the wedge before any further build.

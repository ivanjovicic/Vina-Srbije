# Product Spec — Validation Baseline

## Status

Pre-MVP hypothesis. Not authorized for full implementation.

## Product thesis

Serbian winery discovery is already well served. The strongest unproven opportunity is to connect the physical winery visit to a durable digital relationship:

`discover/visit -> save exact wine/vintage -> consent -> later reorder/action -> measurable winery ROI`

## Primary jobs under validation

### Winery

Acquire a measurable qualified guest and convert tasting visitors into repeat customers without adopting heavy foreign DTC software.

### Visitor

Reliably choose a winery experience, remember exactly what was tasted, and later find or buy it again.

## Target pilot segments

- tourism-ready family/small-medium wineries;
- Fruška Gora/Srem and Šumadija/Topola clusters;
- domestic weekend visitors;
- selected foreign visitors;
- post-tasting consumers.

## Proposed MVP — only after gates pass

- verified winery/experience profiles;
- sr-Latn + English;
- mobile-first responsive web;
- map/search/filter;
- direct/request booking handoff;
- QR Wine Passport;
- wine + vintage save;
- consented follow-up;
- direct reorder/contact link;
- partner claim/verification;
- provenance/freshness;
- basic attributable analytics.

## Non-goals

See README “Do not build yet”.

## North Star candidate

**Confirmed Winery Actions per 100 Qualified Sessions**

Component metrics must remain visible; a composite metric may not hide funnel failure.

## Main unknown

Whether QR/post-tasting behavior creates enough incremental winery revenue or high-intent actions to justify payer willingness.

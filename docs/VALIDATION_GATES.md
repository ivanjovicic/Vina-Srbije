# Validation Gates

No later gate opens automatically because an earlier date passed.

## G0 — Research quality

**Status: PASSED for desk research, real-world evidence still open**

Requirements:
- competitive baseline;
- macro/market baseline;
- legal/payment risk map;
- assumptions/evidence ledger;
- current source review.

## G1 — Winery pain / supply

**Status: OPEN**

Pass when:
- 20–30 qualified winery interviews completed;
- >=5 credible pilot commitments;
- target acquisition/booking/repeat problem appears repeatedly without prompting;
- onboarding/data-maintenance owner identified for pilot partners.

Kill/pivot signal:
- <5 commitments;
- pain consistently low;
- partners only want free publicity/listing.

## G2 — Consumer workflow

**Status: OPEN**

Pass when:
- 25+ relevant interviews;
- meaningful multi-source/booking or post-tasting memory/reorder friction;
- concierge test shows >=40% qualified users perform a winery action.

## G3 — QR Passport

**Status: BLOCKED by G1**

Pass hypothesis:
- >=30% exposed tasting guests save at least one wine with visible staff-supported QR;
- <15% save is kill signal for Passport-as-core.

## G4 — Repeat purchase

**Status: BLOCKED by G3**

Pass hypothesis:
- meaningful 30/60-day return;
- >=10% qualified reorder action click;
- >=3–5% confirmed order/serious inquiry is promising at pilot scale.

Thresholds are hypotheses and must be recalibrated with sample size.

## G5 — Winery monetization

**Status: BLOCKED by G1/G4**

Pass when:
- at least 5 wineries agree to a real paid model after attributable value is demonstrated;
- acquisition/onboarding payback is acceptable.

## G6 — Transactional booking

**Status: BLOCKED**

Requires:
- booking-request demand proven;
- partner response SLA acceptable;
- legal tourism role reviewed;
- named payment/acquiring path;
- cancellation/refund ownership clear.

## G7 — Commerce

**Status: BLOCKED**

Requires all:
- >=50 monthly real reorder/purchase intent events;
- >=10 participating sellers;
- viable money/fiscal model;
- provider confirmation;
- courier/age/breakage economics;
- positive unit economics without unrealistic shipping subsidy.

Test single-winery checkout before any multi-winery cart.

## G8 — Native mobile

**Status: BLOCKED**

Requires:
- retained web cohort;
- mobile-specific scan/push/offline need;
- expected retention uplift justifies second client surface.

## G9 — Regional expansion

**Status: BLOCKED**

Requires:
- repeatable Serbian acquisition;
- >=20 active ROI-positive/paying winery relationships;
- stable data ops/economics.

Cross-border alcohol commerce has a separate country-by-country legal/tax gate.
